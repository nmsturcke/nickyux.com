From support member to co-owner

Six years of progression.

I joined Elite Scrims as a support member, helping players in tickets and answering their questions. Over six years of volunteering, my responsibilities expanded across the platform. Today, I’m a co-owner.

During that time, the community grew from approximately 200,000 members to 1.3 million. That is the platform’s collective growth; my contribution spans the systems, staff, partnerships, and decisions supporting it.

Building the infrastructure.

I built a website and multiple systems integrating with third-party APIs. These tools make staff workflows easier and improve the player experience, including new capabilities that help distinguish the platform from competitors.

Running the operation.

My role extends into staff management, logistics, and the practical coordination behind the platform. Having started in player support, I bring direct experience of the questions and friction that players and staff encounter.

Partnerships & direction.

I’ve been an essential part of securing partnerships with companies including Razer and Logitech, alongside helping decide the community’s future direction. Co-ownership means connecting those longer-term decisions with the people and systems that make them possible.
