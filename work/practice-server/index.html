The starting point.

The team had a vision for the experience they wanted to offer. I worked with the COO and CEO to understand that intent and collaboratively design the user flow, suggesting improvements along the way.

My responsibility.

My role connected product collaboration, systems architecture, third-party integration, and frontend implementation. I worked with the executives as well as the website designer and builder.

Designing the system.

I architected the system around the agreed user flow and connected it to Outseta, a third-party user management platform. That brought the business requirements and the technical integration together in one implementation.

Business requirements User flow System & integration Frontend experience

Delivering together.

I created frontend components and worked with the website designer and builder to keep the experience consistent. The work required considering how backend behaviour, the interface, and the wider site fit together.

Experience developed.

The project combined stakeholder collaboration, user-flow design, architecture, and delivery across disciplines. It demonstrates the kind of work I’m moving towards: understanding the intent and helping the people and systems around it connect.
