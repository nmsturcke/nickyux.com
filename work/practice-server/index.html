Practice Server

Turning an executive team’s vision into a connected product experience.

My roleFreelance engineer
FocusProduct collaboration & systems architecture

Understand the vision.

I worked with the COO and CEO to understand what they wanted to achieve and collaboratively design the user flow. My role included helping translate their vision into a practical experience, while suggesting improvements along the way.

Design the system.

I architected the system around that flow, connecting it to Outseta, a third-party user management platform. The work brought the business requirements and technical integration together in one implementation.

Connect the experience.

I also created frontend components and worked with the website designer and builder to keep the experience as consistent as possible. That meant considering how the backend behavior, interface, and wider site fit together.

Work across disciplines.

This project brought together executive collaboration, user-flow design, backend architecture, third-party integration, and frontend delivery. It reflects how I like to work: understand the intent, contribute to the solution, and carry it through with the people involved.
